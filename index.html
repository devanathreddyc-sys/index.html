About this submission

What is collected here

This page holds two things: the annotated texts I worked with through the semester, and the reflective essay that came out of reading them. Each text below opens as a PDF with my marginal notes, underlining and questions left in place.

The annotations are working notes rather than finished readings — places where a passage caught me, where I disagreed, or where something connected back to an earlier text.
